FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions on Back Office Support

What tasks are included in TEKJEE BPO back office support services?

Our back office support covers daily transaction and register reconciliation, shift audits, EBT and credit card batch settlements, fuel delivery margin auditing, vendor bill approvals, inventory entry, and customer support coordination.

How do TEKJEE transaction reconciliation services eliminate cashier shortages and over/short variances?

We link directly with POS databases (Verifone, Gilbarco, Clover, Square, Toast) and merchant portals to reconcile sales line-by-line against actual bank deposits daily, flagging anomalies and void patterns instantly.

Why do US and UK businesses partner with back office outsourcing companies like TEKJEE?

Outsourcing back office operations reduces overhead by up to 60% compared to domestic clerical staff, while ensuring 24/7 turnaround, weekend coverage, and strict SLA compliance aligned with US and UK business hours.

What specialized back office support is provided for gas stations and convenience stores?

We provide dedicated fuel margin auditing, daily shift cashier envelope balancing, lottery scratch-off and online ticket reconciliation, fuel delivery bill-of-lading matching, and automated Modisoft POS sync.

How does TEKJEE manage restaurant back office support services?

We audit third-party delivery payouts (DoorDash, UberEats, Grubhub) against POS rings, reconcile daily server tip payouts, process food and beverage vendor invoices, and track weekly prime costs.

How do warehouse back office logistics support and freight auditing work?

Our logistics specialists match bills of lading against carrier invoices, audit freight detention charges, update WMS receiving logs, and track purchase order variances across US and UK distribution hubs.
