The Challenge of Multi-Site Commercial Operations

Operating gas stations, convenience store chains, retail outlets, or restaurants requires constant supervision. When owners cannot physically visit every venue daily, store standards inevitably drop:

  • Unpunctual Shifts: Employees opening registers 20 to 30 minutes late, leading to lost morning sales.
  • Cash Register Discrepancies: Failure to execute mid-shift cash drops, unrecorded voids, or cash drawer over/short variances.
  • Neglected SOPs: Skipped safety checklists, dirty customer forecourts, and unstocked display aisles.

Hiring a full-time, on-site assistant manager for each venue can cost upwards of $45,000 to $60,000 per location annually. TEKJEE's virtual back office assistant and remote operations managers deliver continuous, camera-verified oversight across all sites at a fraction of that cost.

How Camera-Assisted Remote Facility Management Works

Rather than relying on self-reported staff checklists, TEKJEE's remote operations team leverages your existing IP camera infrastructure and POS software to verify physical compliance:

1. Morning Opening & Shift Presence Verification

At designated opening hours, your virtual manager verifies staff arrival on camera, confirms that doors open on time, and validates register login timestamps against scheduled employee shifts.

2. Real-Time Cash Drawer & Safe Audits

Operators audit register cash drops, safe insertions, and vendor delivery receiving areas, matching line-item delivery tickets with physical stock placement.

3. Daily Facility Condition Scorecards

Every evening, business owners receive a visual scorecard via WhatsApp or email summarizing staff compliance, cleanliness scores, register reconciliation, and maintenance red flags across all locations.
